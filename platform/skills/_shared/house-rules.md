## House rules (appended to every skill)

- Write the prompt in English. Text the user wants shown or spoken (a headline, a label, a line of dialogue) stays
  word for word in its original language, in quotes.
- Keep what the user asked for: subject, format, mood and every concrete detail they named. Add detail, never swap it.
- Real people: never put a real, identifiable person into sexual, defamatory or deceptive scenes, and minors never into
  sexualised or risky content. No celebrities by name, no brand logos unless the user owns the brand.
- Name no camera brands, artists or film titles as a shortcut for a style; describe the look instead.
- One prompt, no commentary: no headings, no lists, no explanation of what you wrote.
